The whole table is getting stronger

The rank-100 cut-off rose 8.2 points in four years (38.9 to 47.1), while the #1 score moved only 2.4. The middle of the list is catching up, not just the leaders.

How the 2025 top 10 got there

IISc has been #1 and JNU #2 in all five years. BITS Pilani made the biggest jump (17 to 7 in 2025), Delhi University climbed from 12 to 5 and Manipal from 7 to 3, while Jadavpur slipped from 4 to 9.

Biggest climbers, 2021 to 2025

UPES (+55 places) and Graphic Era (+50) climbed the most; most of the biggest climbers are private or deemed institutes. Covers the 80 institutes present in both years.

Biggest fallers, 2021 to 2025

Six of the ten biggest drops are state universities, such as Mysore University (19 to 71), Savitribai Phule Pune University (11 to 56) and Calcutta University (4 to 39).

Churn in the top 100

Every year 7 to 9 institutes leave the list and the same number enter, so about 90 percent of the list stays the same year to year.

How long institutes stay on the list

75 of 128 institutes appear in all five years. The other 53 come and go.

The gap is between central institutes and everyone else

Central and national institutes score 6.2 points higher than state universities in 2025 (59.4 vs 53.2). State universities and private and deemed ones sit close together (53.2 vs 54.9). "Government" as a single group hides this.

Share of the top places

At the very top, government still dominates. Among the top 25, private and deemed institutes rose from 8 to 12 places.

Who improved most since 2021

Private universities raised their research score by 22 points (RPC), far more than central institutes (+4). Central institutes gained mainly on teaching (+10) and outreach (+8.5), and their perception score slipped (−1.4).

Research versus graduation outcomes

Each dot is one institute in the selected year. Central institutes cluster at high graduation outcomes; research strength (the vertical axis) is what lifts an institute into the top 10.

What the scores are made of

Teaching, learning and resources (30%)Faculty, students, spending. Central, state and deemed institutes score almost the same in 2025 (67 to 69), so it rarely decides who leads.
Research and professional practice (30%)Publications, citations, patents. Central 40.8, state 29.5. This is the largest single reason central institutes lead.
Graduation outcomes (20%)Exams passed, placement, higher studies. Government institutes lead (central 79.8, state 76.7) over deemed (70.1).
Outreach and inclusivity (10%)Women, regional diversity, disadvantaged students. Private and deemed institutes score higher than state ones.
Perception (10%)Peer and employer surveys. Older central institutes carry reputation: central 40.1 vs state 32.6 in 2025.
